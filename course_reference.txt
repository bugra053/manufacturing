UNITS AND EMPIRICAL EQUATIONS
Do not force a single unit convention onto every empirical model. A numerical coefficient is tied to its equation's input units. The tutorial's main S-7/S-8/S-13 and BU-2/BU-4 substitutions use a,D,s in metres, T in seconds, v in m/s, force in N, torque in N*m and power in W. Feed/depth selection bounds are printed in mm/rev and mm; convert the computed result back to those units for branch verification. Other tables, especially the Kienzle family, explicitly use N/mm^2 and must keep their own convention.
Basic conversions: 1mm=1e-3m; 1mm^2=1e-6m^2; 1kW=1000W; 1min=60s; 1rev/s=60rpm; 1MPa=1N/mm^2=1e6N/m^2; 1kN/cm^2=10MPa. Thus 60*10^7N/m^2=600MPa=60kN/cm^2. Many homework statements print N/mm^2 beside an SI-sized 10^7 multiplier; do not silently treat that as ordinary steel strength. Use a traceably established correction or request the intended unit.
With D in metres and v in m/s, n=v/(pi D) is rev/s. With D in mm and v in m/min, n=1000v/(pi D) is rpm. omega=2pi*n applies when n is rev/s. For n in rpm, omega=2pi*n/60.
Power F*v with N and m/s is W, not kW. Motor input is cutting power/eta. Mass removal A*v*rho is kg/s with SI inputs; multiply by3600 for kg/h. Do not confuse mass rate, volume rate and cutting-layer area.

BASIC TURNING METHOD
For ordinary longitudinal diameter reduction, a=(D0-D1)/2. The chip cross section is A=a*s. Grooving/transverse cutting depth must be established from its geometry, not automatically the same diameter formula.
S-7 HSS speed: v=Cv/(a^x*s^y)*zeta_k*zeta_m*zeta_T.
Main force with S-13: Fv=Ck*a^x1*s^y1.
Machine motor power: PM=Fv*v/eta, or total simultaneous cutting power/eta for multiple tools.
Chip mass rate: m_dot=A*v*rho, summing simultaneous contributions when appropriate.
Choose the actual material/tool/operation/feed/depth branch before numerical substitution. If feed is an unknown, hypothesize a branch, solve, then verify its inequalities; recalculate with the correct branch if it fails.

CARBIDE BRANCHES AND POWER-LIMITED TURNING
For S-8 structural/carbon/alloy/cast-steel longitudinal turning with P25, the nested feed rows are interpreted by the matching tutorial as:
s<=0.3mm/rev: Cv=.9197,x=.15,y=.2,m=.2;
0.3<s<=0.75mm/rev: Cv=.2713,x=.15,y=.35,m=.2;
s>0.75mm/rev: Cv=.1324,x=.15,y=.45,m=.2.
Do not retain the middle branch merely because its printed s<=.75 also contains smaller values; the more specific nested low-feed branch governs those. The reference Rm is75e7N/m^2; apply the provided zeta_mrp=75e7/Rm. For cast iron S-8 uses its own reference hardness and zeta_mrp=(190/HB)^1.25 where specified.
The tutorial uses v=Cv*zeta_k*zeta_mrp*zeta_T/(T^m*a^x*s^y). Follow the actual course form including its stated correction convention; do not remove or add a life/material correction merely to imitate another handbook formula.
With Z the applicable product of corrections and g=a/s:
PM=Ck*Cv*Z*a^(x1-x)*s^(y1-y)/(eta*T^m).
For known g, s=[PM*eta*T^m/(Ck*Cv*Z*g^(x1-x))]^(1/(x1-x+y1-y)), then a=g*s.
If a two-pass case halves a while holding g fixed, s also halves; recheck the coefficient branch. Do not simply halve motor power or keep a branch whose feed condition no longer holds. State power reduction as (PM-PM_new)/PM*100%.
For HSS power-limited turning without the T^m denominator in the selected model, use that actual form, not a carbide equation copied verbatim.

MULTI-TOOL AND TURRET LATHES
Shared workpiece n does not imply identical cutting speeds: v_i=pi*D_i*n. Compute each tool's cutting depth and active length from the actual drawing. In a simultaneous operation, total power is the sum of simultaneous cutting demands, not a sum of separately solved speeds. For combined turning and drilling on a common rotating workpiece:
eta*PM=n*[pi*sum(Fv_i*D_i)+2pi*sum(M_drill_j)].
Thus n has ONE denominator containing all simultaneous demands. Preserve any multiplicities.
Feed speed is n*s. Active cutting time for a tool is its actual contact length/(n*s). Main operation time for simultaneous supports is governed by their schedule/maximum duration, not an unconditional sum; distinguish active tool wear time from total cycle time. Use only stated approach/overrun allowances or those established in the matching instructor example.
Where a common Taylor relation is applicable, v^z*T=constant; compare tools under the same model and actual conditions. A limiting tool is not always just the largest diameter if material/feed/depth/life conditions differ.
For replacement pieces, K=T/t_active_per_piece; the maximum number of COMPLETE pieces is floor(K), unless a different scheduling policy is explicitly requested. Show a continuous estimate separately if useful. Do not round a tool-capacity limit upward.

DRILLING METHOD
BU-1 feed interpolation inside the actual diameter/material bracket:
s(D)=s_min+(D-D_min)*(s_max-s_min)/(D_max-D_min).
For BU-2 use v=Cv*D^x0*mu0/(T^m*s^y0), with speed n=v/(pi D).
BU-4 torque M=CM*D^x*s^y; axial force Fa=CF*D^x1*s^y1. Steel exponents are x=2,y=.8,x1=1,y1=.7; cast-iron exponents are x=1.9,y=.8,x1=1,y1=.8, as actually printed.
For z identical simultaneous drills, PM=z*M*2pi*n/eta. Together with the tool-life speed limit:
s=[PM*eta*T^m/(2*z*CM*Cv*D^(x+x0-1)*mu0)]^(1/(y-y0)).
This derives from equating the power-limited and life-limited speeds; do not lose the parentheses in the exponent 1/(y-y0) or the drill count z. Here z is tool multiplicity, not the unrelated life-root index used in some turning tables.
Use the problem's eta, even if the worked slide substitutes another one. For the single-drill task2.1.1, the statement eta=.78 overrides the worked numeric .8.
Machining time is actual drilled/active length/(n*s), including only justified allowances. Three simultaneous holes do not mean three sequential tool-life durations per workpiece for each drill. Differentiate parts, holes, tools and groups explicitly.

MULTI-SPINDLE HEAD AND LENGTH-BASED TOOL LIFE
For a common axial head, n_i*s_i=c must be identical after synchronization. The lowest admissible individual feed speed limits the common head speed; preserve different feeds/diameters unless the actual machine imposes identical n and/or s.
Distinguish tool life T in time from cumulative drilled travel L. For the worked2.3.1 interpretation, T=L/(n*s), and substituting this into the BU-2 model gives:
v_L=[Cv*D^(x0-m)*mu0/((L*pi)^m*s^(y0-m))]^(1/(1-m)).
After selecting the common c, n_i'=c/s_i. The resulting cumulative tool travel is:
L_i=[Cv*D_i^(x0-1)*mu0*(n_i')^(m-1)/(pi*s_i^(y0-m))]^(1/m).
Do not copy the repeated identical adopted-regime rows on tutorial p57. The exact statement L=2m also overrides a later '2mm' phrase. For14mm tools use D=.014m, not .0014m.
Total axial thrust is sum(z_i*Fa_i), and total motor power is sum(z_i*M_i*2pi*n_i/eta) with the correct group multiplicities. Calculate each distinct diameter separately.

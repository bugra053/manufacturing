1.2.1 Task

with a diameter of 70 mm. It is necessary to determine the relevant regime of machining so that the
durability of the most endangered knife is T=30 min and after how many machining operations dulled
knives should be replaced.

- Work piece material Structural steel, Rm=60 x 107 N/m2 ;
-Tool material: High-speed steel;
-Feed, s=0,35 mm/rev

2. Drilling – Homework
2.4 Exercise
2.4.1 Task
When drilling on a tower drill, it is necessary to determine the appropriate machining parameters and the
required power of the machine's drive motor such that the drill bit becomes blunt after 50 consecutively
drilled holes.
Input data for the calculation:
- Workpiece material: structural steel / carbon steel, Rm=60 x 107 N/mm2 ;
- Drill bit diameter: 20 mm;
- Drilling depth: 60 mm;
- Efficiency rate of machine transmission, η=0,8 .

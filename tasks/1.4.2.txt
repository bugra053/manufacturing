1. Turning – Homework
1.4 Exercise
1.4.2. Task
Determine the tool life required to machine 50 workpieces by turning on a universal lathe. Based on the
calculated tool life, determine the complete machining parameters.
Input data for the calculation:
- Workpiece material: structural steel, Rm=60 x 107 N/mm2 ;
- Tool material: hard metal / carbide tool P25;
- Diameter before turning is D = 98 mm, and after machining the diameter is d = 82 mm;
- Chip slenderness coefficient: g=8;
- Attack angle of the tool blade: κ=90° ;
- Turning length 50 mm.

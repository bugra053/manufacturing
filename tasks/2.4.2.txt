When drilling on a radial drilling machine, it is necessary to determine the appropriate machining
parameters and the number of holes to be drilled before replacing a dull drill bit, subject to the condition of
fully utilizing the machine's drive motor power and the requirement that the primary machining time per
hole be 42 seconds.
Input data for the calculation:
- Workpiece material: structural steel / carbon steel, Rm=60 x 107 N/mm2 ;
- Drill bit diameter: 20 mm;
- Drilling depth: 60 mm;
- Power of the driving electric motor of the machine is P = 2 kW;

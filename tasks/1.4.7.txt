1. Turning – Homework
1.4 Exercise
1.4.7. Task
On a turret lathe with a vertical head, longitudinal turning and drilling are performed in parallel at the same
time.
Input data for the calculation:
- Workpiece material: Cast Iron, HB=190 ;
- Turning knives material: hard metal / carbide tool K20;
- Drill bit material: high-speed steel;
- Workpiece diameter before turning 100 mm, workpiece diameter after turning 95 mm ;
- Drill bit diameter 20 mm;
- Turning length 80 mm, and drilling depth 50 mm;
- Durability of the more vulnerable tool 30 mm;
- Efficiency rate of machine transmission, η=0,75.

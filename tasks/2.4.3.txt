2. Drilling – Homework
2.4 Exercise
2.4.3 Task
Determine the tool life of a drill bit required to machine 50 workpieces—performing a simultaneous drilling
operation of three Ø20 mm holes (3 x Ø20 mm) on a multi-spindle drilling machine—before becoming
blunt. Based on the calculated tool life, determine the complete machining parameters and the absorbed
drilling power.
Input data for the calculation:
- Workpiece material: CrNi steel, Rm=70 x 107 N/mm2 ;
- Panel thickness 65 mm

1. Turning
1.1 Universal lathes
1.1.1 Task
The workpiece with a diameter of Ø63 mm before machining and Ø60 mm after machining is processed on
a universal lathe.
It is necessary to calculate:
-cross-sectional area of ​​the cutting layer;
-cutting speed for tool durability T=90 min;
-main cutting resistance;
-power of the driving electric motor of the machine;
-the mass of the chip removed per unit of time.
Input data for the calculation:
-Work piece material CrNi steel , R =70 x 107 N/m2
m
-Tool material: High-speed steel;
-Feed, s=0,3 mm/rev ;
-Efficiency rate of machine transmission, η=0,8.

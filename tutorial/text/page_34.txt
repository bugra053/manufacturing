1. Turning – Homework
1.4 Exercise
1.4.1. Task
Rough turning is performed on a universal lathe, reducing the diameter from Ø60 mm to Ø54 mm over a
turning length of 45 mm.
It is necessary to determine the following, assuming the tool becomes dull after 50 produced parts:
- the appropriate machining regime;
- the necessary power of the driving electric motor for performing this operation;
Input data for the calculation:
- The material of the workpiece is CrNi steel, Rm=70 x 107 N/m2;
- Both tools material: hard metal / carbide tool P25;
- Efficiency rate of machine transmission, η=0,8.

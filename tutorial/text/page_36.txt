1. Turning – Homework
1.4 Exercise
1.4.3. Task
The finishing machining of the bore in a bushing—clamped in a chuck and having an outer diameter of Ø50
mm, an inner diameter of Ø40+0,01 mm, and a turning (clamping) length of 150 mm—is performed on a
universal lathe without prior rough machining.
It is necessary to determine the appropriate machining parameters such that the deformation (deflection) of
the workpiece during machining does not exceed the width of the tolerance zone, and the maximum
roughness of the machined surface does not exceed 12.5 µm.
Input data for the calculation:
- Workpiece material: structural steel, Rm=60 x 107 N/mm2 ;
- Tool material: High-speed steel;
- Tool durability/life : T=60 min;
- cutting depth: 1 mm;
- nose radius of knive: 0.5 mm;
- Attack angle of the tool blade: κ=90° .

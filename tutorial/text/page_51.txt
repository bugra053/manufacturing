2. Drilling
2.3 Drills with multi-spindle head
2.3.1 Task
During machining on a multi-spindle drilling machine, six holes are processed simultaneously: two Ø6 mm
(2 x Ø6 mm), two Ø9 mm (2 x Ø9 mm), and two Ø14 mm (2 x Ø14 mm).
Input data for the calculation:
- Workpiece material: structural steel, Rm=60 kN/cm2 ;
- Durability of the most vulnerable drill bit L=2 m;
- Efficiency rate of machine transmission, η=0,8 .
It is necessary to calculate:
- Machining parameters;
- Tool life/ durability for all tools;
- Required axial force to be overcome by the unit;
- Power of the unit's electric drive motor.

2. Drilling – Homework
2.4 Exercise
2.4.7 Task
On a drill with a multi-spindle head, 8 holes are processed simultaneously, namely: 3 x Ø12 mm with a
depth of 45 mm, 2 x Ø15 mm with a depth of 35 mm and 3 x Ø18 mm with a depth of 40 mm.
It is necessary to determine the following, assuming that all drill bits become dull after 50 workpieces
have been produced:
-the applicable machining parameters;
-the power of the machine's drive motor;
-the main machining time.
Input data for the calculation:
- Workpiece material: CrNi steel, Rm=70 x 107 N/mm2 ;
- Efficiency rate of machine transmission, η=0,75 .

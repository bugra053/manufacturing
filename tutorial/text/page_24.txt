On a multi-cutting lathe, the given workpiece is processed by the simultaneous action of all the knives
placed on the longitudinal tool support, as shown in the following machining plan:
The relevant regime of machining is determined from the condition that the
durability/stability of the most endangered knife is T=30 min. The knife No. 1 is
the most vulnerable, because compared to other knives, for the same feed and
cutting depth, it machines the largest diameter, which has the highest cutting
speed and thus the lowest durability/stability.
Determining the relevant machining regime starts from defining the cutting depth.
Cutting depths are calculated based on the difference in diameter before and after
machining for each knife separately. In this case, the cutting depth is the same for
all knives and amounts to:a=5 mm.

1.3 Turret lathes
1.3.1 Task
The workpiece according to the sketch given below is
machined on a turret lathe with a horizontal working head,
so that the longitudinal and transverse supports of the tool
work simultaneously.
It is necessary to calculate:
- the relevant machining regime;
- main machining time;
-main cutting resistance;
- durability/stability of the most endangered tool.
Input data for the calculation:
- the material of the workpiece is cast iron, HB=200;
- the material of the drill bit is high-speed steel, and the
material of the turning knives is hard metal K20;
- power of the driving electric motor of the machine is
P =15 kW;
M
-Efficiency rate of machine transmission, η=0,75.
